Using the app

Use BimmerVision only with vehicles, recordings and accounts you are authorized to use. Follow applicable recording and privacy laws, and operate in-car controls only when safe. You are responsible for choosing storage, protecting your accounts and maintaining independent copies of recordings you need to keep.

Optional services

Google Drive viewing and backup are optional and require your authorization, appropriate Google storage, connectivity, and service availability. Automatic and direct car uploads require eligible car Wi-Fi and vehicle power. Selected website uploads require your Wi-Fi confirmation for each attempt; the browser cannot verify Wi-Fi or enforce the network connection. Phone-assisted backup separately requires explicit consent to use the phone’s internet connection, including possible cellular data and charges. Playback and downloads can also use cellular data. You are responsible for checking your connection and network provider’s charges. Google services are subject to Google's own terms. Backup is not complete until the applicable size and checksum verification succeeds; do not assume a recording is backed up solely because it is queued or a phone transfer has finished. The car independently verifies phone-assisted transfers later on eligible Wi-Fi. If a page or connection stops, remaining files stay queued, and an unfinished phone-created file may restart when the car takes over. Cancelling a transfer or disconnecting an account does not remove copies already stored in Drive. Keep independent originals until you confirm a usable backup. Features can be unavailable or interrupted, and the software is supplied under its included license without a guarantee of continuous operation or recovery of every recording.
