BimmerVision Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026

BimmerVision records supported vehicle cameras and metadata to your USB drive. BimmerVision Web lets you view those files in your browser. Optional Remote Backup copies eligible recordings directly from the car to the Google Drive account you connect.

Recordings and local playback

Recordings can include camera footage, recording times, GPS coordinates, routes, vehicle telemetry and supported perception data. These files stay on your USB drive unless you choose a transfer, download, export or Remote Backup. The web player processes recording files locally; BimmerVision does not operate a server that collects your footage.

Optional Google Drive backup

When you connect Google Drive and enable backup, the car sends eligible recording files and their associated metadata directly to Google Drive. Files are stored in your Google account and use its storage quota. Car backup sign-in, DNS and file-transfer requests use Wi-Fi only, with encrypted HTTPS and no cellular fallback. Wi-Fi is a connection type; your network provider's charges and policies still apply.

BimmerVision requests Google's drive.file permission to create and manage files used by this app. This permission does not grant general access to unrelated files in your Drive. Google provides account information, such as your email address or display name, and storage-quota information so the app can display the connected account and detect a full Drive.

Local account information and credentials

The car stores authorization tokens, the account label, backup preferences, upload progress and completion records locally in files protected by operating-system permissions. Backup preferences can include the Wi-Fi connection identifier. Tokens allow backup to resume without asking you to sign in every time. The tokens are not uploaded to BimmerVision servers. Operating-system file protection is not a claim of encrypted storage.

Your controls and deletion

You can disable automatic uploads, limit backup to recordings from fully locked drives, cancel account setup, change the connected account or disconnect Google Drive. You can also revoke BimmerVision's access in your Google Account. Disconnecting or unlocking recordings does not delete copies already stored in Google Drive; manage those copies, including Drive trash, in Google Drive. Local recordings follow your lock, deletion and storage-retention choices.

Other network providers

Google receives sign-in and Drive requests when you use Remote Backup. Encrypted DNS requests for backup endpoints are sent to Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 service. Online maps in playback request OpenStreetMap tiles; these requests can reveal the map area viewed and your network address, but do not send recording files to the map provider. Website hosting and these services process requests under their own privacy policies.

Limited use

Google user data is used to provide the backup and account-management features described here. BimmerVision's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact and changes

Questions about this policy or BimmerVision's Google access: ghostpixelapps@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
