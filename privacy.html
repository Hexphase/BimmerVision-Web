BimmerVision Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026

BimmerVision records supported vehicle cameras and metadata to your USB drive. BimmerVision Web lets you view local recordings and recordings in your own connected Google Drive account. Optional backup copies selected or eligible original files to that account, directly from the car, from selected local files in the website, or through your phone with separate consent. Phone-assisted uploads require confirmation that the phone uses its own cellular data or another non-car connection, not BMW hotspot internet. The browser cannot force that route; users must verify it before starting.

Recordings and local playback

Recordings can include camera footage, recording times, GPS coordinates, routes, vehicle telemetry and supported perception data. These files stay on your USB drive unless you choose a transfer, download, export or Remote Backup. The web player processes recording files locally; BimmerVision does not operate a server that collects your footage.

Optional Google Drive backup

When you connect Google Drive and enable backup, the car sends eligible recording files and their associated metadata directly to Google Drive. Files are stored in your Google account and use its storage quota. Car backup sign-in, DNS and file-transfer requests use Wi-Fi only, with encrypted HTTPS and no cellular fallback. Wi-Fi is a connection type; your network provider's charges and policies still apply.

BimmerVision requests Google's drive.file permission to create and manage files used by this app. This permission does not grant general access to unrelated files in your Drive. Google provides account information, such as your email address or display name, and storage-quota information so the app can display the connected account and detect a full Drive.

Google Drive viewing and downloads

After you explicitly continue with Google and authorize your account, the hosted website lists BimmerVision recording files available to this app in that account. It reads recording manifests to build the library and downloads video and mapping data when needed for playback, caching, or an export you request. Processing happens in your browser. The Drive viewer is read only: cloud locking and deletion are unavailable. Playback and downloads use your browser's internet connection and may use cellular data; check your network and data allowance.

USB lock controls

With a supported native folder picker, Lock and Unlock ask for write permission and update only the selected recording’s manifest on your USB drive. Video stays unchanged. Browsers using compatibility folder selection remain read only; connect to the car or use a supported browser to save locks. Failed or denied saves do not display a successful lock.

Selected website uploads

Website backup uploads only the recording you select, or all completed recordings in a drive you choose, from a local USB or folder source, including its capture archive, manifest, and available original mapping file. Each upload requires a fresh confirmation that the device is connected to Wi-Fi. This is your confirmation, not an enforced network restriction: browsers, including Safari, cannot reliably verify Wi-Fi or force a particular network, and an incorrect confirmation or later connection change can result in cellular use. The website checks original file sizes and checksums and uses resumable transfers. Cancellation can leave files already transferred in your Drive.

Phone-assisted backup

Phone backup is a separate, explicit option. The car sends bounded original-file chunks over the paired local connection to the hosted website on your phone. The phone uploads those chunks directly to Google Drive using the same Google account as the car's backup settings. Before each attempt, you must consent to use the phone's internet connection, which may use cellular data and incur charges. The browser cannot force cellular routing or verify which connection the operating system uses. The car's own internet requests continue to require eligible Wi-Fi; this option does not enable cellular uploads by the car.

The paired car stores the selected recording list, remote file and folder identifiers, resumable-session information, and transfer progress so remaining work stays queued if the page stops. Browser-created sessions are not trusted as proof of a complete backup. The car independently verifies remote sizes and checksums when it has eligible Wi-Fi. An unfinished phone-created file may restart when the car takes over.

Local account information and credentials

The car stores authorization tokens, the account label, backup preferences, upload progress and completion records locally in files protected by operating-system permissions. Backup preferences can include the Wi-Fi connection identifier. Tokens allow backup to resume without asking you to sign in every time. The tokens are not uploaded to BimmerVision servers. Operating-system file protection is not a claim of encrypted storage.

The hosted website uses Google’s browser token model. Its Google access token and connected-account information stay in page memory; the website does not store that token in local storage or session storage and does not receive a Google refresh token or client secret. Reloading or closing the page ends that website session, and an expired token requires sign-in again. Disconnecting or changing the account cancels active browser transfers and clears that page’s connection. Phone backup sends the car its verified account email, file identifiers, resumable-session addresses, and progress, but never the browser’s Google access token.

Your controls and deletion

You can disable automatic uploads, limit backup to recordings from fully locked drives, cancel account setup, change the connected account or disconnect Google Drive. You can also revoke BimmerVision's access in your Google Account. Disconnecting or unlocking recordings does not delete copies already stored in Google Drive; manage those copies, including Drive trash, in Google Drive. Local recordings follow your lock, deletion and storage-retention choices.

Other network providers

Google’s sign-in script loads on the website only after you choose to continue with Google. Google receives connection information when that script loads, and receives account, library, upload, playback, and download requests when you use the corresponding features. Recording transfers go directly between your device or car and Google; BimmerVision does not relay your recordings through its own server. Encrypted DNS requests for backup endpoints are sent to Cloudflare's 1.1.1.1 service. Online maps in playback request OpenStreetMap tiles; these requests can reveal the map area viewed and your network address, but do not send recording files to the map provider. Website hosting and these services process requests under their own privacy policies.

Limited use

Google user data is used to provide the recording library, playback, download, backup, and account-management features described here. BimmerVision's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
